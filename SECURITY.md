# Security Policy

Grimdall is a security product, so integrity matters.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a vulnerability, **do not open a public issue**.

Email: security@grimdall.com

Include:

- What you found
- How to reproduce it
- Why it matters
- Any logs or screenshots you can share safely

## Review policy

- Changes to the policy engine require careful review.
- Secret handling and redaction logic is security-sensitive.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and triage within one week.
